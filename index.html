Folding Dice Quiz — Guide & Shortcuts

1. The 6 Die Symbols

Each random 6-square net contains all 6 geometric symbols on crisp white squares:

  • + Plus Sign
  • □ Empty Square
  • ■ Filled Black Square
  • ○ Empty Circle
  • ● Filled Black Circle
  • ⚃ Four Dots

2. Non-Folding 2D Visual Hints

Need help seeing how the flat 2D squares meet in 3D without folding the net?

• 📐 Corner Hint: Highlights the 3 colored lines (Cyan, Amber, Pink) and vertex dot that meet at the 3D corner.

• 🎨 Opposite Pairs: Color-codes the 3 pairs of opposite faces (OPP A, OPP B, OPP C) — two opposite faces can never touch!

• 🔗 Zip Edges: Shows which open perimeter edges (E1 ↔ E1, etc.) zip together when folded.

• ❌ Eliminate 1: Crosses out an impossible 3D option and explains why.
