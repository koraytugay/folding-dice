How to use Folding Dice 3D

1. Build Your 2D Net (Up to 6 Squares)

Add Squares: When the net is flat in 2D, hover over any dashed + slot around your existing squares and click to attach a new square (up to 6 squares total).

Delete Squares: Switch to the Delete tool or hold Shift + Click a square to remove it.

Set Base Square: Use Set Base to choose which square stays anchored flat on the ground while all other squares fold up around it!

2. Fold & Unfold Step-by-Step into a 3D Dice

Fold Next Step: Click Fold Next Step (or press →) to slowly fold one square 90° along its hinge crease.

Fold Out Step: Click Fold Out Step (or press ←) to unfold one square back toward the 2D surface.

Interactive Scrubber & Auto-Play: Click any step pill, drag the timeline slider, or click Auto Fold 3D to animate the entire sequence!

3. Spatial Reasoning Quiz Mode (Q)

How to Play: Click Quiz Mode in the top bar. A random 6-square 2D net with the 6 geometric symbols (+ Plus, □ Empty Square, ■ Black Square, ○ Empty Circle, ● Black Circle, ⚃ Four Dots) is generated alongside 4 possible 3D cube perspectives (A, B, C, D).

Non-Folding Visual Hints: Pick and choose any visual hint on the flat 2D net (or press H for Next Hint): 📐 Corner Hint (highlights the 3 lines meeting at a 3D corner in each direction), 🎨 Opposite Pairs (color-codes the 3 pairs of opposite faces that can never touch), 🔗 Zip Edges, or ❌ Eliminate 1!

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • → / ← Fold / Unfold Step
  • Q Toggle Quiz Mode
  • H Next Quiz Visual Hint
  • F Auto Fold / Unfold All
  • 1–4 Tool Modes
  • S Toggle Face Symbols
  • L Toggle 3D Face Labels
  • X Toggle X-Ray Mode
